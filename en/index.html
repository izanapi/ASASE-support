ASASE studio.

A music app for shaping terrain, weather, key, scale and timbre. Available for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  1. Tap “▷ Play” to start. “Pause” pauses the music.
  2. Tap the key or scale name to choose and lock it.
  3. Choose a NEXT option to reserve the next place, key and scale. Tap “Next place →” once to move in 15 seconds, or again while waiting to move immediately.
  4. Use Journey to adjust transitions, and Sound to adjust timbres and individual parts.

AUv3 on Mac

The Mac app also works as a generator in Logic Pro. Follow the illustrated Logic Pro guide. Other hosts depend on their AUv3 support.

ASASE timer.

An iPhone timer with music that changes as you listen.

TIMER
Time until playback ends: 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or Unlimited.
SHIFT
The shared interval for terrain, weather, key and scale transitions: 3, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes. Terrain or weather may sometimes remain unchanged.
PLAY / PAUSE
Start or pause music and the countdown. Music fades out over the final 8 seconds.
STILL
Time since your last touch while this app is in the foreground. It resets when you leave or return. It does not measure inactivity across your device while locked or in other apps.

Music and the countdown continue while the screen is locked or another app is visible. Manual pause, audio interruptions and headphone disconnection stop playback. This is not an alarm that continues after the app is force-quit.

Music is generated offline. The configured ad-supported release displays a banner; loading an ad or consenting to advertising is not required to use music or the timer.

Common questions

No sound, or playback stopped

Check the app and device volume, headphones and Bluetooth output. After a call or other audio interruption ends, tap Play to resume. If the issue continues, save your work and restart the app.

How do I change the language?

English and Japanese are supported. The app follows your device or app language preferences. Reopen the app after changing its language in OS settings.

The timer. banner is not showing

Availability depends on your connection, consent choices and ad inventory. Music and the timer remain available. Where required, use “Ad privacy options” in the app information screen to change your choices.
