Use in Logic Pro

ASASE studio. for Mac includes a standalone app and an AUv3 generator. There is no separate plug-in download. The screenshots below show Logic Pro in Japanese; the menu positions are the same in English.

Get started

  1. Install ASASE studio. on your Mac and open the standalone app once.
  2. Open Logic Pro and create a Software Instrument track in an empty project.
  3. Click the instrument slot in the inspector on the left. If another instrument is already loaded, click the right edge of its slot to open the selection menu. Press I if the inspector is hidden.
  4. Choose AU Generators → ASASE → Asase Studio → Stereo. If “Asase Studio” appears under Recents, you can choose it there as well.

ASASE generates sound on its own. You do not need MIDI notes. To play piano over it, add a separate instrument track.

If it does not appear

Quit and reopen Logic Pro. Then open Logic Pro → Settings → Plug-in Manager, select the manufacturer “ASASE”, and look for Asase Studio. Check that it has a compatibility checkmark. If it is listed but absent from the instrument slot, select it and click “Reset & Rescan Selection”.

You may see other plug-ins under the same manufacturer. Choose the one named “Asase Studio”.

Playback

Use the ASASE window to change terrain, weather, key, scale and volume. Turn on “Sync to Logic / host” to follow Logic’s transport position and tempo. Save your Logic project and reopen it to check that audio and settings return.

If you need help, contact support with your Logic Pro and macOS versions, the Plug-in Manager status and steps to reproduce the issue.
