Privacy policy

Applies to ASASE studio. and ASASE timer.
Developer: izanapi
Effective and last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Music and permissions

Music is generated on your device. The apps do not send audio, listening time or journey settings to the developer's servers. No account is required. The apps do not request microphone, photo or device-location permissions.

2. Information on your device

Information needed for app features, such as Studio journey settings and the previous starting state in timer., is stored locally. It is not sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes its local information. Information included in OS backups follows your backup settings.

3. Advertising in ASASE timer.

ASASE studio. does not include an advertising SDK.

ASASE timer. includes Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform. In the configured ad-supported release, Google may process IP addresses and approximate region inferred from them; device- or app-related identifiers; ad impressions and interactions; app interactions; and crash and performance diagnostics. These may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service improvement. Processing depends on SDK, device, consent and advertising settings.

The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed.

Google consent messages are shown where required. When a privacy-options entry point is required, “Ad privacy options” appears in the app information screen. Music and the timer remain available if you do not consent or an ad cannot load. For Google's handling of information, including retention and international processing, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising

4. Apple services

App Store purchases, downloads and diagnostics shared with Apple follow Apple's services and your settings. The apps do not include separate developer analytics or login services.

5. This website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no custom ads, analytics or contact form. GitHub may process information needed to provide and operate its services.

Support uses GitHub Issues. Posts are public. The developer uses their contents to respond, investigate problems and improve the apps. Do not post personal or confidential information. Information on GitHub is also managed through GitHub's features and policies.

GitHub General Privacy Statement

6. Changes and questions

When features or information handling change, this page and its update date will be revised. For questions, use the support contact without including personal information.
